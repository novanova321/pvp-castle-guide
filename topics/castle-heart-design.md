## Castle Heart Design

Castle Heart design focuses on the elements or the lack of elements around the Castle Heart that aims to help the defenders interrupt anyone trying to key it.

## Endgame

Obviously, the reason that the attackers are on the Castle Heart is because the defenders failed to prevent a breach and failed on preventing the attackers from advancing through the castle. The winner is usually decided at this stage, thus why I call it the endgame. In the endgame, the intentions of both sides can be surmised as following:

- Attackers wants to do everything in their power to be able to interact with the Castle Heart uninterrupted for 10 seconds and click the button to disable defenses, effectively keying it and winning the raid.
- Defenders wants to interrupt anyone interacting with the Castle Heart until time runs out or until they somehow eliminate all the attackers.

With these intentions, the endgame is usually defenders hiding in doors and periodically going out to hit anyone near the Castle Heart while the attackers do their best to eliminate the defenders whenever they come out or distract them long enough to key the castle.

## L Stairs that wraps the Castle Heart

The simplest and the most common design. The idea is to force a turn on an otherwise straightforward route of the attackers to the Castle Heart. Aside from lengthening the path to the Castle Heart, the corner and elevation that the stairs provides should help the defender survive whenever they go out and interrupt the keyers.

The problem with this is it creates a corner where rat form can be harder to see. The same corner is also a difficult position to hit someone and safely get back without taking much damage. Whereas you can passthrough a straight stairs easily while still interrupting the keyer.

## Jungle Design

This design involves sandwiching the Castle Heart with 2 straight stairs while adding as much stairs as possible and extending the safe places from 1st floor to the upper floors. Each floor has multiple balconies to watch over the Castle Heart while allowing the defender to simply press Shift to easily jump down and interrupt any keyer.

This design focuses on helping the defenders be elusive by spamming various shift+jumps from the different levels of the design. When used correctly, the attackers will be forced to clear all doors from all levels to eliminate all safe places.

As of writing, I think it's the best design for Castle Hearts. [Watch the proof of concept](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYrvmhwpv9M)
