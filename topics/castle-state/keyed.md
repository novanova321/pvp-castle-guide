## Keyed State

- Lasts for 10 minutes
- also called "Castle Defenses Inactive"
- Coffins will not work
- Teleporters will not work for owners but will work for everyone else. If you wish to go to the top floor fast, use bat form.
- Castle Waygates that are not broken can be used to get out of the castle **but not** to get in
- All walls and doors will be disabled[^1]
- Storages are open to everyone

[^1]: Disabled walls/doors means you can go through them.
