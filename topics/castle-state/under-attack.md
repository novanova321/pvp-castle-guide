## Castle Under Attack state

- Lasts for 1 minute
- Prevents all castle modifications (dismantle, repair, place)
- Prevents recalling servants

Castle under attack state can be triggered by the following scenarios:

1. siege golem hits any part of your castle including stone stairs
2. enemy hits a tomb or servant coffin or statue or work station or hedge or waygate or any movable structure with hitbox except Target Dummies.
3. enemy hits the castle heart
4. enemy hits an _isolated_ pillar[^1]

[^1]: An isolated pillar is a pillar that is not connected to any wall or door. Empty door frames and entrance blockers does not count as a connection.
