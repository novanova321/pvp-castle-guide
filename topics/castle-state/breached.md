## Breached state

- Lasts for 7 minutes
- Prevents use of teleporters
- All your walls, doors, entrance blockers and pillars take 400% increased damage from <ins>any kind of attack</ins>
- As a defender, you must do everything you can to prevent the enemy from breaching your castle
- As an attacker, your first goal is mostly _likely_ to get a breach
