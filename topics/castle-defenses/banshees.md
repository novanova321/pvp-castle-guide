## Banshees

Requires 15 minutes per banshees. The time can be adjusted by the server settings called `RefinementRateModifier`.

On plots with 2 levels, it is best located right after the stairs that goes to the 2nd level. You want to make it hard for the attackers to fight them. The stairs will make a natural funnel which makes it easy for banshees to hit their fears. It is also possible to setup a [trap](../entrance-designs.md#stairs-corner-trap) if the enemy decides to jump on the side of the stairs.

If plot has only 1 level, banshees are best located near the castle heart to serve as a warning that the enemy are close to the castle heart.

Banshees fits on a 2x4 or a 2x2+2 room. Grouping them together in one big room makes them more effective compared to dividing them into two rooms of 6.

Same with all mobs in the game, you can scale the banshees by having them aggroed by multiple online players. As a defender, you should try to avoid clearing your own banshees. Just say hi and scale them along with the attackers.

Strongest banshee room is the one with Stygian Circle spawning Matka.

Banshees reduce the depth of a castle by at least 1 due to its room requirement.

## Killing Banshees

- Anything with chaos
- Static
- Heartstrike
- Major Explosives

Keep moving around. Don't stay in one area. Don't go back to the area you once were. You either keep holding S, or keep dodging in a circular fashion. Don't use skills with high cast time.

## Relocating Tombs after Spawning

After spawning banshees, you can move the tombs but the banshees will stay where they were spawned. This allows you to have multiple banshee rooms but the room with tombs will only start spawning when the original banshees die.

On servers with accelerated refinement rate, having the original banshees fight at the start then having the tombs spawn at a deeper room is a neat trick. It's almost similar to doubling your tombs.

- If refinement rate is 1x, you need 30 minutes between the 2 banshee rooms to make it effective.
- If refinement rate is 2x, you need 15 minutes between the 2 banshee rooms to make it effective.
- If refinement rate is 3x, you need 10 minutes between the 2 banshee rooms to make it effective.

Having the tombs placed on a deeper room also means you won't be exposing the tombs for an easy trigger for Castle Under Attack state.
