## Stygian Circle

Best used before breach. Hostile to all parties, don't use if not familiar. Best located in a room where you will be able to pinch the siege golem.

You can lure the blood golem or boss to finish off the low doors that the attacker prepared for rebreach.

## Blood Golem

When sleeping, it does not give you combat debuff which means you can heal while moving around the castle to change position. It can stun the Siege Golem. When scaled, it's very hard to kill.

## Primal Boss

On death, primal Boss heals all of its suckers by 200hp, spawn health orbs and replenishes the blood of everyone in the area that are on the same team of the suckers.

Werewolf can stun siege golems.
Angram is the worst if your castle is breached since all Angram's AoE skills will damage all nearby doors/walls further breaking your castle.
Jade is very good if she silences the enemy, very bad if she silences the defenders.
Leandra is decent.
Octavian is decent.
Bane is decent.
Ungora is probably the worst.

## Matka

Best spawned in the same room with banshees. Can turn vampires into pigs.
