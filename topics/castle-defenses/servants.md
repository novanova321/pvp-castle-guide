## Servants

Best castle defense however if they cannot see the enemies, they are useless.

- The higher the blood, the better since servant expertise affects both servant hp and damage
- Revive always takes 10 minutes regardless of room flooring.
- Breaking the coffin only pauses the revive timer, it does not reset it back to 10 minutes.
- Servants can revive even if castle is breached.

## Top-tier Servants

- Alchemist - highest aoe damage, does not hit counter
- Strikers - big damage and the shield blocks melee and projectile hits while also dealing a lot of corrupted damage
- Clerics - big AoE shield, single heal, very hard to kill especially if there's multiple clerics that are healing and shielding each other
- Nuns - best AoE heal. great for surviving a heartstrike
- Militia Veteran - very big burst of damage and stuns if it hits
- Priests - big heals and summons "floating orbs" that can hold players and golems into place
- Vipers - big damage and can throw an aoe on lower levels

## 8 Servants

Quick recommendations:

- 3 alchemists, 2 clerics, 2 strikers, 1 nun
- 4 alchemists, 3 clerics, 1 striker
- 8 militia veterans

## 5 Servants

Quick recommendations:

- 2 alchemists, 2 clerics, 1 nun

## Fire Servants

Never choose a servant that deals fire damage because fire is neutral and burns everything regardless of team. It will just kill the other servants, destroy servant coffins and burn the defenders. Golems also geat 100 fire resistance for free making fire a bad source of damage against it.

## Servant Room

2x2 box. You can use hedges or any decoration to block some areas of the room to prevent servants from idling where they won't see the enemy.

Always check if the door being opened blocks the exit of servants.

### Floor

Crypt flooring <ins>does not</ins> make servant respawn any faster than 10 minutes. It only affects the servant convert rate.

Servants also don't idle on tiles without foundation. You can intentionally remove foundations to prevent them from idling on the far-side of the servant room. To prevent rocks and trees from growing back on the tiles without foundation, you can use growing plots.

### Location

If the attackers has to go straight to get to the Castle Heart, put the servants on the left or right of the [pit](../entrance-designs.md#fighting-pit). Try not to reduce the castle's depth by putting the servant room in the way to the heart.

You never want the servant room too far from the pit that they don't even get aggroed when you open the door.

When breached, it is best-case scenario if the attackers have to do a side quest to stop servants from reviving or to clear them or to expose the coffins for a possible trigger for Castle Under Attack state later on.
