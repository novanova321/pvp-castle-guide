## Main Floor

This is the top floor of your castle where everything that you do not want others to touch is located.

There is no best way to organize the main floor but I'm going to share how I do it.

1. First is the obvious: foundations and walls on the whole top floor. If there is not enough tiles, I will focus the areas above the entrance and above the Castle Heart.
2. Second would be to put a hole to drop the entrance and the Castle Heart. The landing area should be a safe place. The holes will act like a shortcut to get the defenders in both ends of the castle quickly.
3. Using the first hole as a guide, I will then setup the main hall. The hall will contain the following: main waygate, teleporter hub, stone coffins, consumables storages (heals, buff, elixirs/coatings), jewel storages, weapon storage, soul shard storages (optional), personal storages (optional), and dumps[^1] (optional). The size will be between 3x6 or 3x9. Floor will be treasury.
4. I will then surround the main hall with the various rooms needed for a working castle.

> I take pathing very seriously on the main floor. I try to remove any blockers or inconveniences when moving from one room to another. I want the most used paths to be as smooth as possible.

## Usually rooms setups

For Alchemy room, I use a 2x4 room. 2 Advanced Blood Presses on each side then 4 Alchemy Tables in the middle.

For Vermin room, I use a 3x3 then just put 4 Vermin Nests.

For Tailoring room, I will have a room that fits 1 Leatherworking Station, 2 Advanced Tanneries, 6 Advanced Looms, and 4 Tailoring Benches.

For Forge room, the biggest room to fit at least 10 Advanced Furnaces, 2 Anvils, 2 Fabricators, 1 Ancestral Forge and 1 Fusion Forge.

For Workshop room, a room that fits 4 Advanced Grinders and 2-4 Advanced Sawmills.

For Prison room, jewellery room, and storage room, I just wing it.

For Loot room, a room big enough to contain a lot of big containers. Usually with a teleporter so you can go to main hall as quickly as possible.

> It is common to keep adjusting the size of the rooms as needed. If there's a need to make 200 golems, expand the jewellery room as big as possible then just revert it after.

## Important Considerations

- The distance between coffin, heal storage, and the entrance hole should be minimal. In raid defense, every second matters. Whenever you revive, you never want to waste 15 seconds running around grabbing heals and making your way back to the fight. That's also why you want a hole at the back that drops at the Castle Heart. You can put your wood coffins in top floor in the back, close to the hole with some storage for heals and use that coffin whenever you need to be at the Castle Heart as soon as possible.

[^1]: dumps are storages for unsorted items. The idea is after farming, you just <ins>**dump**</ins> your inventory into these containers then continue farming. Sorting of the items will be done in batch instead of sort every time you bring home some loot. Very common in official servers. I usually have 4-10 dump containers and I label them `dump1`, `dump2`, etc..
