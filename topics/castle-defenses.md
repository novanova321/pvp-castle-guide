## Vermin Nests

Buys a bit of time. Broken on servers where the Rat Boss spawns health orbs and heals the players that suck it. If you want to go this route, simply place it near the entrance and suck the boss whenever you need to full heal. Spawning a lot of rat boss can be pretty laggy which can be good if you have the better PC.

## Abomination

Acquired by feeding prisoners with a lot of Irradiant Gruel to turn them into Abominations. Buys a bit of time but I have never seen it actually do anything good.
