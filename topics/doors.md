## Doors

- All doors have 2,500 health
- All door frames have 1,250 health
- **Exposed** door frames can be breached. Consider it a half-hp door when looking for something to breach fast.
- Exposed **wide** door frames cannot be breached and indestructible assuming both end pillars are connected to a wall/door/window. It does take damage and gets destroyed if castle is breached.

## See-through vs Opaque

It's best to use the see-through iron doors for the following reasons:

1. It gives a lot of information which is mostly beneficial to the defenders like deciding whether to wait for a full heal or go out to save a friend or kill an enemy.
2. If you locked a player, defenders can easily identify which door should not be opened.
3. There are plots that can be attacked from multiple sides after a breach and with the see-through doors, you can see those attacks very clearly even if you're far away.

Opaque doors can be used to hide important information like servants reviving.

## Single swing vs double-hinged

Double-hinged door like `Verdant Gate` is better because:

- When opened, it lets players slide through when pushed to the sides while you can get stuck on single swing doors if you are the side where the opened panel is.
- After passing through, you can turn immediately without any issue (like panel blocking you, etc..)

## Servant-locked

<ins>**Always always always use servant locked doors**</ins>. Servants can be lured and tricked to open/close non-servant locked doors.

On doors near the Castle Heart, I often use doors without servant lock. The bright red lock indicator creates a visual clutter and can help conceal a rat, whereas a cleaner-looking door makes them much easier to spot.

> This is one of the most fun way to rob a castle.

## Castle Edges

Always use doors even on the edges of your castle. If you can go in the middle of a door and close it, that's a safe place to fully heal.
