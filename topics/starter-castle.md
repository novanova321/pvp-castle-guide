### Main Plot Starter Castle Design

This design is solely for progressing to the highest possible gear level. It is not for raiding. It's just the one you need before you can build a better one.

At this stage, the castle must be:

1. Airlocked
2. Fully walled up on all sides / 99% secure

## Recommended Room Setups

- 2x3 workshop room (3 grinders, 2 sawmills)
- very big forge room
- 2x2 alchemy room
- 2x3 tailor room
- 2x2 jewellery room
- 3-tile wide treasury room hallway with waygate in the middle
- 3x3 alchemy room for 4 vermin nests
- 4x3 prison room
- starter garden
- room for devourers, servant coffin(s), stables, research stations, eyes, and castle throne
