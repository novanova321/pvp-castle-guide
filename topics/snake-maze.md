## Snake Maze

The path from your 1st floor to your main floor. If you dismantled the teleporters connecting your top floor to the 1st floor, the attackers will be forced to go through your snake maze. How long and how well you make the snake maze directly affects how much time it takes for an attacker to get to your loot.

## Snake Sides

Never use windows. Wall is fine but door is the best.

- Windows allow projectiles to go through it. A skilled raider can skips parts of the maze by abusing this mechanic. Therefore, **do not use windows**.
- Wall has no railings when the castle is keyed allowing the attacker to hit any filler two tiles over. Breaking fills allows the attacker to jump on it and skip some parts of the maze.
- Door keeps the railings when keyed blocking all attacks on the sides and forcing the attackers to go through each tile of the maze. No skips. No shortcuts.

## Snake Fillers

Best way to delay the attackers from getting to top floor even if the castle is keyed. Assuming there's no teleporter that goes up.

- Hedges - most annoying as it completely blocks the way
- Storages - another annoying one especially if some of the storage have contents
- Pools - decent and it prevents frog jumps

## Ultimate Tryhard Form

Snakes made up only by doors and each room is filled with 4 storages and some of them has content.
