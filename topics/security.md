## Secure-as-you-build

Once you start building, never stop building until it's secured. Doesn't matter if you're starting from scratch, just expanding, or remaking your whole castle. Never get distracted by any other task or else you risk going back to a fully looted castle. If you have to quickly leave, at least put doors or wrap everything with walls.

Added a stairs? Wrap both levels with walls and put doors right away. Do it immediately before someone sees your castle is under construction and sneaks in like [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bE640gYWnY4).

Turning a starter castle into its final form can take at least an hour. Prevent your castle from getting jumped on by thieves by proactively encasing the whole castle with walls or closed doors on **ALL LEVELS** as soon as you add something new. This also means each floor of your castle will be secure from the other floors of your castle.

A box castle is the simplest and easiest way to be 99% secure. No side doors, no balconies, no holes, just one entrance and everything is walled up.

## Airlock

When making a starter castle, there should always be a minimum of 2 doors between the outside world and the inside of your castle. At any time of the day, a rat can be hiding at your outmost door waiting for you to open it so they can sneak inside and grab all your loot.

[Here's a video of me setting up an airlock.](https://youtu.be/_Aro0uTvG4o?t=605)

## Teleporters

As soon as you unlock Domina the Blade Dancer, use red teleporter to travel between outside and inside of your castle. There should be no need to open and close doors anymore.

## Palisades / Wood Walls

Replace all of them with stone. No exceptions. You can be breached by exploding 2 major bombs or 4 minor bombs on a palisade wall/door.

## Hire a Thief

Test your security by asking a thief to try and rob your castle.[^1]
