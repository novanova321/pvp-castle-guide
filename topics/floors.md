## Floors

Crypt Flooring for servants room only affects the time it takes to convert a servant. It does not lower the servant's respawn timer of 10 minutes.

Floors near the Castle Heart should be easy on the eyes to spot rats that is trying to key it.

## Colors

On the ground level, you can use floor colors to indicate an information like calling out "Player A locked in red" where "red" is just a room with red floors. Or you can use it identify how deep the enemies are in the castle like "They are on blue floors now".

## Servant Room Foundation

Servants does not idle on tiles without foundation. You can trick the servants to move to a better position by removing select foundations.

## Carpets

Carpets grant vampires a 15% movement speed bonus, making them useful near entrances where quick repositioning can be crucial. However, they come with a significant drawback: if a carpet is connected to an adjacent tile, you cannot build a wall across that connection.

This becomes a problem once a breach timer expires and you need to quickly seal off parts of your castle. Instead of rapidly spamming walls to block attackers, you're forced to place a door frame first and then add a door which is a much slower process that can cost valuable seconds during a defense.

## No Foundation Trap

Outside raid window, you can use Exterior Invisible Foundation to trick the enemy into thinking that there's no foundation by still having trees/rocks in the front of your castle. If a fight near your castle and they decide to push into the plot, surprise them by quickly surrounding the front area with walls to lock them in. Putting a wall or anything when outside raid window makes it instant and indestructible. Exterior Invisible Foundation is unlocked by killing Polora.

> I love this trap. I do it all the time and it's very effective.
