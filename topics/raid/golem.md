## Basic Golem Infos

- Needs <ins>2 minutes 30 seconds</ins> to be ready
- Spawned golems despawns after 10 minutes
- Can be ridden for 5 minutes
- Hitting a player riding a golem does not give PvP combat debuff but taking damage from a golem does[^0]
- Can cancel skill animations as soon as it deals the damage
- Earthshatter (R) is the skill with the longest cast range
- Earthshatter (R) can go through ponds (for some reason)
- You can interact with the Castle Heart as a golem and there won't be clear animation for it. Regardless, you are stil 1 hit away from being interrupted.

## Stats that work while riding a golem

### Physical, Spell, Health & Healing

Physical power and spell power is **locked to 50**. Golem does not crit. Attack speed does not work because each golem auto attack has a cooldown of 0.8s. Health and healing affects the vampire inside the golem but does nothing for the actual golem hp.

### School Mastery

These are the buffs you get the more spells you unlock for that spell school.

- [x] Chaos Magic 1 - Increases veil cooldown rate by 5% for Roar (Space).
- [x] Illusion Magic 1 - Increases spell cooldown rate by 5% for Earthshatter (R).
- [x] Illusion Magic 2 - Increases movement speed while shapeshifted by 6%.
- [x] Frost Magic 1 - Amplifies the effect of all shields applied to you by 8%.
- [x] Frost Magic 2 - Increases all resistances by 10. [^2]

### Stats Overview

- You can get as much as **+44% shapeshift speed** if you have Feral Haste, Mutant 1, Creature 2 and Sanguine Mastery.
- You can get as much as **+31% golem shield hp** if you have Scholar 1, Chill Weave and Sanguine Mastery.
- Parry and damage reduction is just as important.
- Additional shields does not stack. The shield from golem is always the one that takes damage first.

## Golem Rider Recommendations

If your sole purpose is to be the golem rider, I recommend this [Creature Brute 2 build](https://www.v-arena.com/builds/create?build=244444444bcfnal2567j2358534625t3E38a076Ec079Cm3E83n5168o3E83h3E68d0E8741111322):

- brute parry is very good versus burst damage
- creature gives you the most chance to kite and survive so you can keep riding more golems

[^0]: You can wait for a full heal while still continuously dealing damage to the golem.

[^1]: Enemy castle is also considered an enemy. Walls/doors/anything will take the extra damage.

[^2]: Riding a golem locks your sun and fire resistance to 100. This only increases your resistance to Garlic, Holy, and Silver.

[^3]: There are very few instances where a golem can get crowd controlled.

[^4]: There are no plots that gives Vile Corruption stacks. It is only at Stavros, Lucile, Dantos and Megara areas.
