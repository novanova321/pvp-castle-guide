## Basic Infos

1. All doors, stone walls, and stone windows have 2500 hp but narrow stone door frames only have 1250 hp.
2. Wood walls, doors and door frames only have 500 hp.
3. Wide door frames does not take damage unless the castle is breached. [^1]
4. Every structure slowly regenerates hp.

## Castle States

If you castle is in any of this state, that's not good.

- [Castle Under Attack](../castle-state/under-attack.md)
- [Breached](../castle-state/breached.md)
- [Keyed](../castle-state/keyed.md)

[^1]: Breaking the supporting pillar breaks all empty door frames attached to it.
