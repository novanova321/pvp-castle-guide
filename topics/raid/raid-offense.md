## Raid Offense

For me, raiding is the ultimate test of skill in V Rising. It's where strategy and decision-making matter more than mechanical execution. No other activity in the game involves as many variables or demands as much adaptation. Compared to defending, raiding is significantly more challenging, requiring careful planning, coordination, and the ability to overcome whatever obstacles the defenders have prepared.

## Breachables

To get a breach, you have to **destroy** one of this:

1. wall - includes window walls
2. narrow door frame[^1] - where you attach your doors and entrance blockers
3. door - this includes wide doors
4. entrance blocker - the iron things that looks like prison bars that cannot be opened

If it's made up of wood, you can breach using:

- 4 minor bombs
- or 1 major bomb
- or a siege golem

But if it's made up of stone, you have to use a **siege golem**.

Since siege golem is a hard requirement for stone castles, focusing on destroying spawned golems before they can ride it is a very strong defense strategy.

## Breach Point

aka initial breach target. It's best to avoid the door that is very close to the stairs or the funnel. The idea is to allow your team to go in and out without taking collateral damage from the attacks that are being directly at you.

It's also good to choose a corner where you can do [splash damage](#golem-splash-damage) to have multiple breach points.

## Typical Raid Flow

1. <ins>**Get a breach**</ins>
2. <ins>**Dig into the castle until castle heart is exposed**[^2]</ins>
3. <ins>**Key the castle**</ins>

## Clearing Servants

Get the servants out of the way as soon as possible.

Place 3-5 siege golems. Send one golem in for the sole purpose of baiting the servants to come out. They take 10 minutes to respawn and revive can only be started if the castle is not on any state.

Counterplays:

- Release the servants to push the attackers out so you can repair. Don't release if not needed. Keep track of the door hp.
- Start revive as soon as possible

## EZ Key

Straight to the final step. If you can find a way to get to the castle heart with a key without getting a breach, that's the easiest way to win. This means you always have to inspect the castle you're raiding before you actually do it.

Counterplays:

- [Secure your castle](security.md)
- Hide near your castle heart, wait for the enemy to start keying then kill them. Free key.

## EZ Breach

Fastest way to do step 1. Find an exposed wood wall / door / door frame, [breach it](breached-state.md#breachables).

Counterplays:

- [Replace all wood things with stone](security.md#palisades)

## Golem Splash Damage

When using the golem's Earthshatter (R) up close, you can hit multiple doors at once. This lets you have multiple [breach points](#breach-point). Hitting a combination of doors and walls is also ideal since it takes longer to repair various types of breachables and most of the time it is overlooked.

Counterplays:

- Remember the corner in which the golem is attacking from and check the hp of all doors close to it. Make sure to repair all of it instead of just the outmost door.
- Use a counter whenever the golem is using Earthshatter (R).

## Hit and Run

The safest and surest way to get a breach. Do some cheap damage on the door but never stay long enough to break the golem. When golem shield hp is below half, simply swap to a new one then repeat until castle is breached. Best done after clearing servants. The key here is consistency.

Having a lot of Shapeshift Movement Speed helps.

Some players do 1 combo of Slam (Q), Stomp (E) and Earthshatter (R) then run outside while some only do one Stomp (E).

Counterplays:

- Release servants, chase then either go for a repair or wipe the attackers.

## Golem Train

Best way to brute force a breach. Spawn a ton of golems, have a lot of golem riders and go in 1 by 1 without stopping until castle is breached. This is the more aggressive version of [Hit and Run](#hit-and-run) since it requires more golem riders. Best done after clearing servants.

If the defenders are lacking damage, you can go in with multiple golems at once.

Counterplays:

- Kill the golem riders or destroy the spawned golems before they can ride it.

## Raid Shortcut

A shortcut is a shorter route to get to the castle heart. It usually involves attacking from the side or from the back and jumping your way into those positions. You can do a temporary castle relocation if the shortcut requires a neighboring castle.

How it usually goes: Force a breach. Use the shortcut.

Not all castles have a shortcut. Do this if you are bored of the normal way of raiding or if you are just feeling crafty and want to show-off. Parkouring your way into the castle heart can be fun.

Also, a shortcut can be made from mistakes with the castle construction. Knowing the exact distance of Arctic Leap, frog jump and mist trance helps identify shortcuts as they appear.

Shortcuts are harder to pull off in 2v2s since it's easier to track everyone's whereabouts.

Counterplays:

- Be aware of the possible shortcuts in your castle. Keep track of attackers. Try your best to prevent it.
- If you catch a player doing a shortcut, they most likely have a key on them so try to focus and kill that player first.

## Raid Supply Stash

It is common for raids to fail due to lack of golems or the defenders fully repairing while attackers are busy getting more supplies. The idea is to look for a nearby hidden place then drop a red bag full of raid supplies. Red bag lasts for **45 minutes**. Instead of travelling all the way home to resupply, you just go to your hidden red bag.

This is a very risky play that really depends how good the hiding place is.

Counterplays:

- If you feel like the attackers are re-supplying too fast, try checking some nearby corners and maybe you'll find a red bag.

## Rat Keying

Best way to key a castle is to hide your presence first. You do not want to be seen and interrupted as it takes 10 seconds to finish interacting with the castle heart. Transforming to a rat hides your health bar and makes your model very small.

Counterplays:

- Use floors that allows you to see rats easily (avoid using dark colored floors around the castle heart)
- Heigthen your hearing. If you hear someone transforing into a rat, quickly clear the area around castle heart.

## Common Attacker Misplays

- Letting them repair for free
- Not preparing multiple doors for rebreach
- Not preparing golems for rebreach
- Bringing a key too early and losing it
- Not respecting the servants
- Pushing and getting locked
- Not checking the state of the spawned golems
- Getting lost around the castle and breaking the wrong doors

[^1]: Destroying a door frame by breaking the supporting pillar does not count. You have to target the door frame only.

[^2]: Dig means to destroy everything in the path to the castle heart. Rebreach when necessary.
