## Hiding Loot

Mostly done in official servers when the defenders cannot see a way to win or intentionally giving up the castle.

## Vampire Box

**Cannot be looted.** 14 inventory slots. 1 per castle. On squads, a player can have 2 castles. If you have a squad, split into 4 solos and have 2 Castle Heart each. That's 4 \* 2 \* 14 = 112 inventory slots.

## Loot Locking

Turn surplus resources into non-lootable assets. If you have an excess of Reinforced Planks, spend them on empty containers rather than storing them directly. You can dismantle the containers later and recover the materials whenever you need them, regardless of whether your castle was raided.

Note that converting loot to wall or pillar decorations may not end well since walls and pillars are destroyable.
