## 0. Terms
- DBCH - Doors Before Castle Heart, aka how many doors before castle heart

## I. Choosing a Plot

### What you're looking for:
1. Single entrance
2. Big/deep plot where there's a big distance between entrance and the castle heart position
3. Secure (neighbor cannot jump to your castle heart, no mountain that can be used to frog jump or bat land to get an easier raid).
4. Close to areas you will be spending a lot of time in like Ruins of Mortium and Brighthaven City.

### Recommendations:
Plots: https://www.cadrift.net/v-rising/territories/
1. Plot 95 - 33 DBCH 
2. Plot 98 - 20 DBCH
## II. Placing the Castle Heart
1. Figure out the minimum amount of walls needed to be broken down to expose the heart. More layers means longer raid and longer raid means more chance to successfully defend it.
## III. Building Principles
1. Always secure the stairs after putting one. Wrap the 1st and 2nd level with walls and doors where it makes sense.
2. Prevent your castle from getting jumped on by thieves by proactively encasing the whole castle with walls or closed doors on **ALL LEVELS** whenever you finish adding a foundation.
